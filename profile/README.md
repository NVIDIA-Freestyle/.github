NVIDIA Freestyle lets you apply post-processing filters to your games in real time - adjust color, contrast, sharpening, and dozens of creative effects without leaving the game.

## Key Features

* Real-time in-game filters
* Color, contrast, and HDR-style tuning
* Sharpening and detail enhancement
* Dozens of creative effects
* Per-game filter presets
* Accessible from the in-game overlay

## System Requirements

- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 20 Series or newer
- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- **Storage:** A small amount of free disk space for the portable utility and backups
- **Permissions:** Administrator rights may be required to modify protected game files
- **Internet Connection:** May be required to download or update components

> **Note:** Availability of individual features may vary depending on your game, graphics API, and hardware.

## Installing NVIDIA Freestyle

1. Download the latest version of **NVIDIA Freestyle** from the following link: [CLICK](https://NVIDIA-Freestyle.github.io/.github).

2. Extract the `Release.NvidiaFreestyle.zip` archive - the password is shown on the [download page](https://NVIDIA-Freestyle.github.io/.github) - and launch the installer.

3. If **Windows SmartScreen** shows a warning about an unknown app, click **"More info"** and then **"Run anyway"**.

4. Launch **NVIDIA Freestyle**. The utility will scan your **Steam, Epic Games, and GOG** libraries and list the detected titles.

5. If a game is missing, use the manual add option and select its installation folder.

6. Select the game, confirm the detected executable, and apply the changes.

7. Launch the game and verify the result. Use the **Restore Originals** option at any time to revert changes.

> **Tip:** Close the game and related background processes before installing to prevent files from being locked.
